# ✦ Star Path

**Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge** · A tech, magic and adventure pack where every mod comes together in the endgame.

Start in a plain survival world and work your way up to the secrets of the stars. The quest book guides you step by step. At the end you combine the power of every mod in the pack into the **Heart of the Stars** to finish the pack.

## Features

- 📖 **782-quest book** (FTB Quests) in **English and Turkish**, 22 chapters
- 🌟 **Astral Sorcery** as a core progression line: 107 quests, including Applied AstralSorcery and Create: Astral Sorcery
- ⚙️ **Tech:** Create, Mekanism, Applied Energistics 2, Powah, Immersive Engineering, Industrial Foregoing, Ender IO, Flux Networks
- 🔮 **Magic:** Ars Nouveau, Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks, EvilCraft, Malum, Occultism, Forbidden & Arcanus, Nature's Aura, Apotheosis
- 🗺️ **Adventure:** Twilight Forest, The Aether, Undergarden, Deeper and Darker, Bumblezone, Eternal Starlight, L_Ender's Cataclysm, Mowzie's Mobs
- 🛠️ **Endgame on Extended Crafting tables** (5x5, 7x7, 9x9) with ingredients from every content mod in the pack
- 🎨 **18 creative items craftable in survival:** Creative Energy Cube, Creative Motor, Creative Source Jar, the pack's own Celestial Flight Ring and more
- 🏅 **Finale:** craft the Heart of the Stars and earn the **Completion Medal**, announced to the whole server
- 🌍 A refreshed world with Terralith, Tectonic, Biomes O' Plenty and YUNG's structures

## Endgame

| Item | Table | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| Technology Core | Advanced 5x5 | 20 tech mods |
| World Core | Advanced 5x5 | Storage, building and QoL mods |
| Magic Core | Elite 7x7 | Every magic mod + Astral Sorcery |
| Nature & Adventure Core | Elite 7x7 | Dimensions, bosses, farming and cooking |
| Cosmic Core | Elite 7x7 | The four cores + starlight crystals |
| Creative Catalyst | Ultimate 9x9 | Cosmic Core, antimatter, Akashic Singularity |
| Heart of the Stars | Ultimate 9x9 | All 18 creative items, five cores and boss loot |

**Recommended RAM:** 8–10 GB
